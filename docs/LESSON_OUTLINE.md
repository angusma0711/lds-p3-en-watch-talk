# LESSON_OUTLINE — LDS-P3-EN-01 P3 English Watch & Talk (Animals / pets)

**status:** drafted  
**Project:** LDS-P3-EN-01  
**Owner:** Learning Designer → Angus review via Studio Coordinator  
**Audience:** HK P3, very weak English; one teacher + one (or small group) learner  
**Duration:** ~25–35 min  
**Language default:** Traditional Chinese UI / scaffolds; English target words & educational terms kept in English  
**Practice note:** Practice-informed classroom pattern only. Does not claim proven / evidence-based outcomes. No prices, clients, testimonials, research citations, or identifiable student data.

---

## 1. Learning outcomes (observable, modest)

By the end of the session, the learner can:

1. **Point / tap** the correct picture for at least **4 of 6** target animals when given the English word or a short prompt (dog, cat, mouse, bird, fish, lion).
2. **Match** each English animal word to its Traditional Chinese gloss (狗 / 貓 / 老鼠 / 鳥 / 魚 / 獅子) with app support (ZH hint, graphics).
3. **Say or complete** the frame `I have a ___` with one target animal word (oral or tap-to-complete), after hearing a model.
4. **Complete** 1 exit-ticket star item in the app (local session only) that shows they can choose the animal that matches a short English cue.

*Not claimed:* fluency, spelling mastery, grammar explanation, or transfer beyond this lesson’s items.

---

## 2. Prerequisites

- Learner can sit and watch a short YouTube song (~3–4 min) with teacher nearby.
- Learner can tap / click large picture buttons on a tablet or laptop (fine motor OK for large targets).
- No prior English required beyond willingness to listen and point.
- Teacher can open YouTube + one app URL; no marking load expected.

## 5. Teacher moves vs learner moves (per phase)

### Phase 1 — Watch (4–6 min)
| Teacher | Learner |
|---|---|
| Open video; full screen if possible; volume comfortable. | Watch / listen; may point at screen animals. |
| One short line before play: 「我們看一首歌，裏面有動物。」 | No writing. |
| Do **not** pause to teach grammar mid-song unless learner is distressed. Optional: one replay of a short segment only if needed. | Stay with teacher; headphones optional. |

### Phase 2 — Warm-up vocab (5–7 min)
| Teacher | Learner |
|---|---|
| Open app → Start → Vocab cards. Sit beside learner. | Tap through 6 cards; look at picture + English + 中文. |
| Prompt once if stuck: 「按下一張。」 / 「聽一聽。」 | Optional tap-to-hear if available. |
| Do **not** quiz orally yet; let app carry the cards. | No need to write. |

### Phase 3 — Check (6–8 min)
| Teacher | Learner |
|---|---|
| Stay nearby; monitor; do not mark by hand. | Answer 4–6 items (picture choice / yes-no / match). |
| If frustrated: 「可以看提示。」 (ZH hint). | Use ZH hint / try again; see instant feedback. |
| Celebrate effort briefly: 「好。」 — no long English correction speech. | Continue until items done. |

### Phase 4 — Say it (5–7 min)
| Teacher | Learner |
|---|---|
| Open sentence frames; model once if app audio off: *I have a dog.* | Complete 2–3 frames (`I have a ___`) by tap and/or short oral repeat. |
| One stuck prompt only: 「跟著說：I have a…」 then wait. | Optional tap-to-hear model. |
| Do **not** explain *a/an*, plurals, or pet vs wild. | Prefer short production over perfect accent. |

### Phase 5 — Exit (3–5 min)
| Teacher | Learner |
|---|---|
| Open exit ticket; one star item. | Complete star item; see Done screen. |
| Note privately (optional teacher notebook — **no student name in app**): roughly “got it / needs redo”. | Session stays on device (local only). |
| Close tab when Done; no cloud save, no photo of face/name. | Finished. |

## 8. Accessibility notes

- Prefer **large buttons**, high contrast pictures, one question per screen.
- Allow **repeat audio** and **ZH hint** without penalty messaging.
- Avoid timed countdown; learner goes at own pace within lesson window.
- Colour is not the only cue (label with word + picture).
- Sitting height / glare: teacher adjusts device; landscape OK if buttons stay large.
- If learner has hearing difficulty: rely on picture match + teacher mouthing the frame once; song still useful as visual story.

---

## 9. Non-technology fallback (app or network fails)

1. Teacher still plays video if YouTube works; if not, teacher shows paper cards and **says** animal names + sound words once each.
2. **Paper cards:** 6 pictures (dog/狗 … lion/獅子). Learner points when teacher says English word.
3. **Oral check (4–6):** “Is this a cat?” (yes/no) / “Point to fish” / match English word strip to picture.
4. **Say it:** Teacher models *I have a dog.* Learner repeats with one card.
5. **Exit:** Teacher shows 2 pictures, says one English word; learner points. Teacher keeps only a private tick (no named digital record required).

---

## 10. Differentiation for very weak learner

- Stay on Vocab cards longer; allow **second full pass** before Check.
- Use ZH hint freely; accept **pointing** as success on Check if tap is hard (teacher taps for them after they point).
- Reduce Check to **4 items** if fatigue shows.
- Say-it: only **one** frame (*I have a dog* or learner’s favourite animal from song).
- Exit: accept picture choice without oral English.
- Praise attempt; never force full sentence if distress rises — return to pointing.

## 12. Pedagogy choice (for THIS need)

**Multimodal input (song + picture + ZH gloss) + controlled production (fixed sentence frame).**  

Weak P3 English learners often cannot hold open listening *and* open speaking. The song supplies memorable sound–animal pairs; pictures and 中文 cut translation load; the app’s short closed items give practice without teacher marking; the single frame `I have a ___` lets the learner say something English-shaped without inventing grammar. Teacher load stays light so an exhausted teacher can still run the session. This is a **practice-informed** fit to the constraint — not a claim of a fashionable method or researched efficacy.
